# 🤖 Vision-Based Multi-Tasking Robot


## 🏆 Achievement

> 🎉 **Proud Finalists of the Sri Lankan Robotics Challenge (SLRC) 2026 – University Category**

Our team successfully advanced to the **Final Round** of the **Sri Lankan Robotics Challenge (SLRC) 2026**, one of Sri Lanka's most prestigious university robotics competitions organized by the **Electronic Club, University of Moratuwa**.

The competition challenged teams to develop a **fully autonomous vision-based robot** capable of solving multiple real-world and simulated tasks simultaneously, including:

- 👁️ Computer Vision & AprilTag Detection
- 🧭 Autonomous Maze Navigation
- 📦 Object Detection, Pickup & Storage
- 🤖 Robotic Arm Manipulation
- 🌐 Real-time Communication with a Simulated Robot
- ⚡ Parallel Task Execution
- 🎯 Precision Autonomous Navigation

---

## 🚀 Competition Tasks

Our autonomous robot was designed to complete several challenging tasks during the competition:

✅ Navigate a complex maze autonomously

✅ Detect and decode AprilTags using Computer Vision

✅ Extract navigation coordinates

✅ Identify and collect colored cubes

✅ Store collected objects onboard

✅ Repair the Particle Laser by inserting cubes

✅ Communicate with the virtual robot (Ares)

✅ Navigate both physical and virtual environments simultaneously

✅ Complete hidden challenge on the final day

These tasks required integration of **Computer Vision**, **Robotics**, **Embedded Systems**, **Motion Planning**, and **Real-Time Decision Making**.
